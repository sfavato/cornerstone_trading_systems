# Subscription Tiers & Quotas

To ensure fair usage and maintain system performance, access to certain features is governed by a quota system tied to your subscription tier.

## Feature Access by Tier

| Feature               | Freemium | Pro       | Quant     | Lifetime |
| :-------------------- | :------: | :-------: | :-------: | :------: |
| Dashboard Access      | ✅       | ✅        | ✅        | ✅       |
| Trading Pairs         | Beginner | Top 50    | All       | All      |
| Discord Bot (`!gem`)  | ❌       | 15 / mo   | 40 / mo   | Unlimited|
| Pine Script Generator | ❌       | ❌        | ✅        | ✅       |

***

### ⛔ Quota Reached

If you exceed the monthly limit for a feature (e.g., the `!gem` command in Discord), the bot will respond with a "⛔ Quota Reached" message. Your quota will automatically reset on the first day of the next calendar month.
