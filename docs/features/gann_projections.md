# W.D. Gann Technical Analysis: Beyond Indicators

Our system goes beyond basic technical indicators by integrating the analysis principles of W.D. Gann. This methodology, based on the relationship between **Time and Price**, allows us to identify support and resistance zones with greater precision and to add a crucial validation layer to our harmonic signals.

## Identifying Major Market Swings

The first step in our Gann analysis is to identify the **"Major Swings"** of the market – the most significant turning points on a chart. These points are not just "highs" or "lows," but structural pivots that dictate the underlying trend. Our algorithm is trained to spot these swings to anchor our analysis.

## Projecting Support and Resistance Zones

Once a Major Swing is identified, we use Gann's tools to project future zones where the price is likely to react.

- **Gann Angles:** We draw angles from the major swing points. These angles are not simple trend lines; they represent **Time-Price relationships**. A 1x1 angle, for example, represents one unit of price for one unit of time. When the price interacts with one of these angles, it signals a potential support or resistance zone.

- **Gann Squares:** We also use "squares" and other geometric tools to project key price levels based on the vibrations of the initial swing.

## A Validation Layer for Confluence

The main objective of our Gann analysis is not to generate signals in isolation. It is a **validation layer** for our harmonic patterns.

When a harmonic pattern forms and its completion point (the D point) coincides with a Gann projection (an angle or a projected price level), the **confluence** is much stronger. This overlap increases the probability of the trade's success.

In summary, Gann's principles allow us to confirm that a harmonic pattern is not forming in a vacuum, but in a structurally relevant area, thus adding significant weight to our **Confidence Score**.
