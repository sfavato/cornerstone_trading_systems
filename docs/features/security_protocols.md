# Security Protocols: "The Shield"

At Cornerstone Indice, we believe that preserving capital is just as important as growing it. That's why we built **"The Shield"**—a layer of defensive algorithms that run 24/7 to protect your portfolio from market anomalies.

> **"Our AI doesn't just seek to win, it is programmed to refuse to lose stupidly."**

## 1. The Crash Kill-Switch (The -3% Rule)

The market can turn in an instant. Our **Crash Kill-Switch** is the ultimate emergency brake.

*   **Trigger:** If Bitcoin (BTC) drops by more than **-3% in a single hour**.
*   **Action:** The system immediately **blocks all new trade entries**.
*   **Why:** A violent drop in BTC often drags the entire altcoin market down with it. By freezing activity, we prevent the bot from "buying the dip" during a falling knife event.

## 2. Weekend Risk Protocol

Crypto never sleeps, but liquidity often dries up on weekends, making the market prone to manipulation and "flash crashes."

*   **Mechanism:** We calculate a proprietary **Weekend Risk Score (WRS)** every Friday.
*   **Action:** If the risk is too high (High WRS), the system activates its "Intelligent Circuit Breaker," reducing exposure or completely pausing trading until Monday liquidity returns.
*   **More Info:** Read the deep dive in our [Weekend Risk Protocol](../pro_journey/cookbook/weekend_risk.md) page.

## 3. Smart Reversal: The Anti-Trap Mechanism

Many trading bots fail because they blindly follow a trend or buy too early.

*   **The Trap:** Buying a dip that turns into a crash.
*   **The Solution:** **Smart Reversal**. The system demands a specific divergence between price and momentum before entering a trade.
*   **Benefit:** This acts as a filter against "fake signals." Even if a pattern looks perfect geometrically, if the Smart Reversal confirmation isn't there, the trade is blocked. It ensures we only enter when the selling energy is truly exhausted.
