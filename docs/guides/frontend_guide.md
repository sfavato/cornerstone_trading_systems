# Navigation Manual: Mastering Your Trading Interface

Welcome to the [**Cornerstone Indice**](https://cornerstone-indice.com) portal.

It is crucial to understand that this interface is **not a simulation**. It constitutes the visual, real-time representation of our Alpha Engine's activity. Every piece of data displayed comes directly from the internal state of our trading servers, offering you radical transparency regarding opportunity detection and risk management.

The interface is structured around three operational pillars.

---

## 1. The Dashboard: The Control Tower

**Objective:** Obtain an instant assessment of the market and system health. This is your daily starting point.

**What you will find here:**
* **Market Synthesis:** The current state of the volatility regime (e.g., "Range", "Trend", "Chaos").
* **Latest Signals:** A direct feed of recently detected patterns.
* **Live KPIs:** A summary of global performance (Profit Factor, Current Drawdown).

> **Go deeper:** The Dashboard integrates fundamental analysis elements to contextualize technical movements.
> 🔗 *See: [Understanding Fundamental Analysis](../features/fundamental_analysis.md)*

---

## 2. The "Signals" View: The Operational Heart

**Objective:** Follow the complete lifecycle of an opportunity, from its algorithmic validation to its financial execution.

Here, the distinction is critical:

### A. `Nearest Trades` (Validated Opportunities)
These are harmonic patterns that have passed our validation filters (high Confidence Score) but have **not yet** reached their entry price.
* **Key Indicator:** The **"Proximity Info"** measures the exact distance (in %) between the current price and the entry point.
* **Action:** Monitoring. The system is armed and waiting for the trigger.
* **The Technology:** These signals are generated by our proprietary detection grid.
    * 🔗 *See: [How HarmoGrid Works](../features/harmogrid.md)*
    * 🔗 *See: [Understanding Gann Projections](../features/gann_projections.md)*

### B. `Active Trades` (Ongoing Positions)
These are positions that have been **executed** by the system.
* **Management:** The monitoring service actively manages these trades (Stop-Loss, Take-Profit).
* **Action:** Performance tracking. Here you see the reality of execution.

---

## 3. The "Performance" View: The Transparency Audit

**Objective:** Verify the promise of "Quantified Trust" through numbers.

Unlike classic platforms that hide losses, we display our entire history to allow for continuous auditing.

**What you will find here:**
* **Equity Curve:** The evolution of capital over time.
* **R-Multiple Analysis:** The distribution of our gains relative to our risks (how many times the initial risk was won).
* **Drawdown:** The precise measure of the maximum risk incurred.
