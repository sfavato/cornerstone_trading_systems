# Quickstart Guide: Your Frontend Interface

Welcome to your trading portal. The website [**https://cornerstone-indice.com**](https://cornerstone-indice.com) is the **visual, real-time representation** of the trading signals generated by our microservices architecture. This is not a simulation site, but a direct interface that continuously reads the state of our database to inform you of opportunities detected and positions managed by our system.

This interface is designed to be your co-pilot, offering you complete transparency into the operations of our Alpha engine.

## The Three Key Sections of Your Interface

Your interface is organized into three main sections, each with a specific role to help you interpret the system's activity.

### 1. The Dashboard: Your Overview

- **Objective:** To provide an immediate summary of the market's state and the system's overall performance.
- **What you'll find there:**
    - The latest validated signals.
    - A summary of key performance indicators (KPIs).
    - The current market state (e.g., volatility regime).
- **How to use it:** This is your daily starting point to get a feel for the market and our Alpha at a glance.

### 2. The "Signals" View: The Operational Heart

- **Objective:** To display in detail the trading opportunities detected and their current status.
- **What you'll find there:**
    - **`Nearest Trades`:** Harmonic patterns that have been validated by our **Confidence Score** but are still waiting to reach their entry price to become active.
    - **`Active Trades`:** Positions that have been executed and are currently managed by our `monitor_trades` service.
- **How to use it:** This is where you track the evolution of opportunities, from detection to execution.

### 3. The "Performance" View: Proof in Numbers

- **Objective:** To offer a detailed and transparent analysis of the system's historical performance.
- **What you'll find there:**
    - Standard performance metrics (Profitability, Drawdown, Profit Factor).
    - Analysis of trades by R-Multiple (Risk-to-Reward), our central metric for assessing the quality of risk-taking.
- **How to use it:** Use this section to audit our results and understand the risk and return profile of our strategy.
