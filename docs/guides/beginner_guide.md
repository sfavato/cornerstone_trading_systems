# 🔰 Understanding the System: Beginner's Guide

Welcome. If you are reading this, you are looking for a smarter, less stressful approach to cryptocurrency trading. This guide simply explains how our technology works and how to use it, without complex mathematical jargon.

## 1. The Philosophy: Why AI?

Manual trading is difficult because it is dominated by two emotions: fear and greed. Most traders lose money not because they don't know how to analyze a chart, but because they crack under pressure.

Our system, Cornerstone/HarmoFinder, was designed to eliminate emotion from the equation. It doesn't "feel" the market; it calculates it.

Imagine an ultra-powerful filter:

*   The market sends thousands of pieces of information (noise).
*   The system analyzes everything 24/7.
*   It only transmits opportunities to you that have a high mathematical probability of success.

## 2. How does it work? (In 3 simple steps)

You don't need to be an engineer to understand the logic. The system works like a safety funnel.

### Step 1: The Radar (Harmonic Patterns)

The system scans charts looking for specific geometric shapes (called "Harmonic Patterns"). These shapes repeat historically and often indicate a price reversal.

*   **Analogy:** It's like a meteorologist recognizing the shape of a cloud predicting rain.

### Step 2: The Judge (The AI Confidence Score) 🧠

This is our "secret weapon". Once a shape is found, our Artificial Intelligence analyzes it. It compares this shape to years of history to answer a single question: *"What is the probability that this trade will be a winner?"*

*   **Score < 60%:** The AI rejects the trade. Too risky.
*   **Score > 75%:** This is a "High Confidence" opportunity. These are the ones you receive.

### Step 3: The Seatbelt (Risk Management)

No system is infallible. That's why every signal includes a strict "seatbelt": the Stop-Loss. If the market doesn't do what is expected, the system cuts the loss immediately to protect your capital.

When you receive an alert (on Discord, Telegram, or the App), it looks like this. Here is how to decode it:

## 4. The 3 Golden Rules for the User

If you follow our signals or do Copy Trading, respect these rules to last:

*   **Never ignore the Stop-Loss:** This is rule #1. The AI defines a precise invalidation level. Never move it out of "hope" that the price will go back up.
*   **Trust the Score:** A score of 80% has a statistically higher chance of succeeding than a score of 65%. Adapt your stake accordingly.
*   **Patience:** The system does not trade all the time. Sometimes, nothing happens for 2 days. This is normal. Not losing money is just as important as earning it.

## 5. Simplified Glossary

*   **Long (Buy):** We bet that the price will go up.
*   **Short (Sell):** We bet that the price will go down.
*   **Harmonic Pattern:** A geometric figure on the chart (e.g., "Bat", "Crab", "Butterfly") that serves as our starting signal.
*   **R:R (Risk/Reward):** The ratio between what you can lose and what you can win. We always aim to win more than we risk.
